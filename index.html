What the warehouse does not hold

  • Water quality. No EPA, USGS or state source yet. Added to the roadmap on 24 September as a domain with candidates (EPA Water Quality Portal, USGS gauges, EGLE beach advisories, GLERL algal-bloom bulletins); nothing verified.
  • Fisheries. NOAA landings and trade are next on the roadmap. Great Lakes commercial fishing data sits mostly with state DNRs and the Great Lakes Fishery Commission. (Aquaculture is on the page — 64 Michigan operations, from the Census of Agriculture.)
  • The rest of the resource map. USDA's rural water program (its FY2025 file is farm payments by the million rows, a server job), partners and programs as curated entities, and SBIR abstracts (the public file has none). Dollars of output and multipliers: a further source and a purchase.
  • Marine forecasts. Access was checked on 24 September: the weather service lists bulletins but does not include their text, so each is a second fetch and the source needs a design decision before it is built.
  • More than nine days of vessel traffic. The AIS archive runs 2015 to five months ago; loading a shipping season is a deliberate backlog item until there is a server to hold it. Every AIS-derived figure here says "nine days".
  • Vessel tracking from lock to berth. The lock log begins August 2026; the AIS archive ends March 2026. No shared day yet.
